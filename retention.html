The topic notes and workbooks are for learning this stuff. Everything here is for keeping it: through all three exams, and for the three years each cert lasts. Pick the right tool for the job: flashcards for facts, the ladder for connections, drills and the miss log for judgment, and write-ups for the things I only think I understand.

The weekly rhythm

  • Daily, 10 minutes: review whatever's due.
  • After every practice session: log the misses, with the why.
  • Weekly: one mixed drill, redo the misses that are due, and read the patterns.
  • Before starting each new cert: walk its rows on the concept ladder, bottom up.
  • After each build milestone: the write-up, from memory first.
  • After passing: flip that cert to keep-warm on the review page.
