Once a cert is passed, its deck shifts to maintenance: no new cards, every interval stretches to at least 30 days, and at most 15 of its cards a day. It keeps AIF fresh while I'm deep in AIP, and all three fresh for the 3-year renewal.

How the schedule works

Leitner boxes. A new card starts in box 0. Good moves it up a box, easy moves it up two, hard keeps it in place for half the interval, and again drops it to box 1 and puts it back in today's queue. Box intervals are 1, 3, 7, 14, 30, and 60 days. Review history stays in this browser.

Cards live in _data/cards/*.yml. Add one there and it shows up here and in the CSVs on the next build.
